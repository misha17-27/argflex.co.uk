SEO for the three new articles

Paste each pair into the Search engines card at the foot of Admin → Blog → the article.

Why these and not the ones already there. With nothing set, the page builds its own title from the headline plus the shop name. That came to 72, 73 and 94 characters, and Google shows about sixty — so the third one was cut off well before it said what the article is about. These are written to fit, and to carry the words a buyer types: hose cut to length UK, measure hose bore, SAE J30 R6 vs R10.
The descriptions are already fixed in code — they used to be chopped at 160 characters mid‑word (“…and what ”, “…does no”) on eleven of the twenty‑three articles. Setting one here still beats the automatic version, because it can sell the click rather than just repeat the opening.
