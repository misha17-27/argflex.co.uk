About Arg Flex Ltd

A concentrated range, held in stock and understood

We are dedicated to providing high-quality solutions for fluid transfer and industrial applications. Rather than listing thousands of lines we cannot supply, we hold a focused catalogue and know every product in it.

Our hoses are engineered for flexibility, durability and resistance to a wide range of chemicals — whether you are transferring petroleum, chemicals or other fluids.

  • Tube, reinforcement, cover and pressure listed on every product
  • Cut to length from 1 m — you are not forced into a full coil
